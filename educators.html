Educator & Administrator Hub

A School-Ready Research Opportunity

Support students with a structured event that strengthens analytical thinking, project planning, and scientific communication.

Pedagogical Benefits

  • Data management through practical, real-world datasets.
  • Time management through milestone-based research planning.
  • Experimental design and method justification.
  • Group coordination and division of responsibilities.
  • Poster presentation and oral defense under formal judging.

The symposium can complement advanced biology, data science, STEM research, and interdisciplinary capstone pathways.

For Schools and Program Leaders

Schools can use this event as an extension of classroom learning or as a culminating showcase for club projects. Advisors may help students frame viable questions, evaluate data quality, and maintain reproducible records.

Because projects are dry-lab focused, participation remains accessible to schools without wet-lab infrastructure.

Suggested Advisor Checklist

Before Registration

  • Confirm student teams and topic interest.
  • Review expected abstract format.
  • Set internal school deadlines.

During Project Phase

  • Track progress on methods and analysis.
  • Monitor quality of evidence and citations.
  • Encourage reflection on limits and bias.

Before Symposium Day

  • Check poster readability and structure.
  • Practice concise verbal summaries.
  • Prepare logistics and travel plans.
