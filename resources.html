Recommended Student Workflow

  1. Choose a biological topic with clear data availability.
  2. Explore relevant datasets and define a focused question.
  3. Analyze with R or Python and document every step.
  4. Draft abstract early, then iterate after obtaining results.
  5. Design a poster that prioritizes claims supported by data.
