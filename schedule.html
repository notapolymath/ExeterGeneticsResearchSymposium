Planning Notes for Delegates

  • Arrive early to complete registration and setup calmly.
  • Bring a digital backup of your poster and abstract.
  • Practice concise explanations tailored to different audiences.
  • Allow time between sessions for networking and reflection.

On-Campus Flow

The schedule balances presentation opportunities with dedicated judging and closing recognition. Delegations are encouraged to plan transitions between Grainger and Forum in advance for a smooth day.
