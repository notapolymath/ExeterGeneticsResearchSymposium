About the Program

Student Leadership with Faculty Mentorship

The symposium is organized by student leaders and backed by academic guidance designed to mirror university-style research culture.

Our goal is to create a rigorous yet welcoming environment where students can practice responsible data-driven biology research and learn to communicate their ideas with precision and professionalism.

Faculty Advisor

Dr. Summer Morrill, instructor of BIO586: Molecular Genetics, guides the program with deep expertise from her research experience at MIT's Koch Institute. Her mentorship ensures strong scientific standards, methodological clarity, and ethical discipline.

Participants benefit from a framework that combines technical practice with careful interpretation and clear communication.

The 12-Month Journey

Fall 2026

Learn the meta-field of biological research and essential dry-lab tools including R, Python, and public dataset exploration.

Winter 2026

Develop independent projects, refine analyses, and finalize abstract submissions before registration closes.

Spring 2027

Synthesize work into professional posters and present findings during the symposium competition.
