Submission Standards, Rubrics, and Awards

This page outlines exactly how to prepare abstracts and posters for fair, high-quality evaluation.

Abstract Requirements

  • Length must be 100 to 300 words.
  • Use a clear research structure: introduction, purpose, method, results, and conclusion.
  • Write for a mixed audience of students, educators, and judges.
  • State limits and assumptions in your analysis when relevant.

Download Abstract Template

Poster Requirements

  • Poster size must be 44" wide by 33" high in landscape orientation.
  • Use minimum 24 pt fonts to ensure readability from distance.
  • Prioritize clear figures, concise text blocks, and informative captions.

Judging Framework

Judges evaluate projects on scientific thought, methodological rigor, clarity of communication, and visual presentation quality. Students are encouraged to explain both findings and limitations thoughtfully.

Preparation Recommendations

  • Rehearse a 2-minute and a 5-minute project explanation.
  • Prepare to justify dataset choice and analytical method.
  • Use consistent visual language across poster sections.
