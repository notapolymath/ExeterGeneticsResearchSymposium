The Spring 2027 Exeter Genetics and Biotechnology Club Research Symposium

Grainger and Forum, Phillips Exeter Academy • Exact Date TBD

A national launch pad for high school researchers. Whether you are brand new to research or already curious about computational biology, this symposium helps students build real dry-lab skills in R, Python, data analysis, and public biological databases.

Register Now Registration is open to students from external schools nationwide.

Mission

We connect motivated high school students with practical research workflows, poster communication, and mentorship by faculty and industry professionals so students can confidently step into modern biology.

Competitor Guidelines

  • Abstract: 100–300 words.
  • Include introduction, purpose, method, results, and conclusion.
  • Poster Dimensions: 44" wide × 33" high (landscape).
  • Minimum font size: 24 pt for readability.

Educator & Administrator Hub

This symposium gives schools a high-impact, project-based research platform. Students develop data management, time management, experimental design, and collaborative communication skills while presenting work in a formal academic setting.

Educators are encouraged to share symposium opportunities with research clubs, science classes, and STEM programs.
