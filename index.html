The Spring 2027 Exeter Genetics and Biotechnology Club Research Symposium

Grainger and Forum, Phillips Exeter Academy • Exact date TBD. Registration is open to high school students from schools nationwide.

This symposium is designed as an academic launch pad for students entering biological research for the first time. Participants build confidence in dry-lab research, data literacy, and computational workflows while learning how to communicate findings to technical and non-technical audiences.

Students will gain guided exposure to R and Python analysis pipelines, public biological databases, reproducible notebook practices, and evidence-driven interpretation. The program emphasizes research habits used in university and industry environments.

Why This Symposium Matters

  • Build scientific reasoning without prior research experience.
  • Translate raw data into a coherent biological narrative.
  • Develop communication skills through poster presentation.
  • Connect with educators, mentors, and peer researchers.

Skill Foundation

Students begin with fundamentals in coding, data structures, statistical reasoning, and scientific literature review.

Independent Inquiry

Each team pursues a focused question, identifies datasets, and documents methods and results transparently.

Academic Presentation

Participants synthesize findings into professional posters and defend their conclusions during formal judging.
